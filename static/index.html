DIZY PLAN · BIRINCHI QADAMLAR

Plan. Izoh. Yangi vazifa.

  1. Plan oching. Namuna planni tanlang yoki DWG/DXF fayl yuklang.
  2. Tahrirlang. Mebelni suring, 90° aylantiring, chiziq yoki to‘rtburchak chizing.
